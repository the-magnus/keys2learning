2. Two forms of textbook

What making.md step 4 offers. The learner's need picks the form: lessons to learn to see in a new way, modules to learn by making something. Shared parts are blue; the parts where the forms differ are green.

Either form: Must cover holds three or four points the source itself stresses; a concept taught in another program is pointed to, not repeated; a module is taken in three parts, listen, build, conclude.
